Make as much silver as you can

You have five copper ingots of… varying quality. Five customers are on their way.

  1. You know each ingot’s true quality. Your customer doesn’t.
  2. Pick a price from 1 to 25 shekels and make one offer.
  3. Too high for them, and they walk out. What they’ll pay depends on two things: how much silver they’re carrying, and how much they trust you. Read their description for clues.
  4. Charge more than the fair price and a complaint tablet follows. Your reputation drops, and later customers will pay less.
  5. Charge more than 50% above the fair price and they return the ingot. You keep nothing.
