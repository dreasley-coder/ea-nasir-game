Ea-nasir (you)

Make as much silver as you can

You have five copper ingots of… varying quality. Five customers are on their way.

  1. You know each ingot’s true quality. Your customer doesn’t.
  2. Pick a price from 1 to 25 shekels and make one offer.
  3. Too high for them, and they walk out. Richer customers pay more, and so does everyone when your reputation is good.
  4. Some customers know copper well. Others can’t tell an ingot from a rock. Overcharge someone who doesn’t notice, and they leave happy.
  5. Overcharge someone who does notice, and a complaint tablet follows. Your reputation drops and later customers pay less. If you charged more than 50% over the fair price, they return the ingot and you keep nothing.
  6. Read each customer’s description for clues about their purse and their eye for copper.
