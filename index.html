Ea-nasir (you)

Make as much silver as you can

Five ingots on your shelf, one of each grade. Five customers on their way.

  1. Tell each customer which grade you’re selling, and name your price.
  2. They accept, make one counter-offer, or walk out if your price insults them. If they counter, you accept or refuse.
  3. Once a price is agreed, choose which ingot actually goes in their sack. It doesn’t have to be the one you promised.
  4. They inspect it. Someone who knows copper may spot a swap: then they complain, or return it if you charged far too much for what they got. Complaints hurt your reputation, and later customers pay less.
  5. Read each customer’s description for clues about their purse and their eye for copper.
